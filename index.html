How to play

Hexadek is a puzzle about sorting coloured tiles. Each cell of the board holds a stack of hex tiles. Your goal is to build stacks of 12 tiles all of one colour, called a hexadek. A finished hexadek clears from the board and scores points.

Stacks

The tiles on top that share a colour are the stack's top run. The symbol on top shows its colour, and the number shows how many tiles are in it. This stack's top run is 3 blue tiles.

Pouring

→
  • Tap a stack, then tap a neighbouring cell, or drag the stack there.
  • Its whole top run moves: onto a stack with the same top colour, or onto an empty cell.
  • Only neighbours: the six cells touching it.
  • A stack holds at most 12 tiles. If the whole run doesn't fit, only what fits moves.
  • Standard game: each pour uses one. You start with 3, get 3 more for each stack you place and 1 for each hexadek, and can save up to 9. Undo gives the pour back.
  • Free game: pours are free, so make as many as you like. Choose the mode under New game.

Placing

  • Below the board are three new stacks to choose from, and a preview of the next one.
  • Drag one onto an empty cell, or tap it and then tap the cell. Placing ends your turn.

Clearing and scoring

12 tiles of one colour clear: 120 points. A second clear in the same turn scores ×2, a third ×3.

The end

Each turn adds a stack, so the board fills up. The game ends when there's no empty cell and no pour can make one.

Tips

  • Pick one or two colours to build up, and give them room: keep their neighbours free for pouring.
  • Use an empty cell to park a top run and uncover the colour underneath.
  • Try not to bury a colour under a different one.
  • Keep two or three cells empty when you can, and check the next stack before you place.
  • In a Standard game, spend pours where they finish or set up a hexadek, and save the rest for a crowded board.
  • Undo is free until you place, so experiment.

Controls

  • Tap or click, or drag. Tapping during an animation skips it.
  • Esc lets go of a selected stack. Ctrl+Z or Cmd+Z undoes a pour.
  • Restart plays the same game again from the start, with the same stacks, to try another plan. New game deals new stacks.
