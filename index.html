How to play

  • Pour: tap a stack, then tap a neighbouring cell (or drag the stack there). Its top run, the same-coloured tiles on top, moves onto a stack with the same top colour, or onto an empty cell. Pours are free.
  • Place: drag a stack from the tray onto an empty cell, or tap it and then tap the cell. Placing ends your turn.
  • Hexdek: a stack holds at most 12 tiles. A full stack of one colour clears: 120 points, ×2 for a second clear in the same turn, ×3 for a third.
  • The game ends when there's no empty cell and no pour can make one.
  • Undo takes back pours until you place. The game saves itself as you go.
